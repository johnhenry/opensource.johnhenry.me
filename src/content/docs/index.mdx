## About

These are personal open-source projects, maintained under
[github.com/johnhenry](https://github.com/johnhenry) and published to npm. New
packages use the `@johnhenry/*` scope; several older ones keep their original
unscoped names.

Commercial products live separately at [erisera.com](https://erisera.com).
